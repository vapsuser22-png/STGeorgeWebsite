Welcome to St. George's School Unit-II, located at Bagh Muzaffar Khan, Agra. Affiliated to the Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations (CISCE), New Delhi (School Code: UP386), we curate an inspiring educational journey of academic mastery, experiential discovery, and character building. With state-of-the-art STEAM laboratories and interactive smart classrooms, we empower students to lead and innovate in the modern world.

Highlights

High School

In the high school years at St. George's School Unit-II, robotics and coding are not just subjects; they're integral to our students' growth as future leaders in technology. Our curriculum empowers them to design, build, and program robots, and to code solutions for real-world challenges. Interactive teaching panels provide insights into advanced coding languages and techniques. Experiential learning takes the form of hackathons, tech symposiums, and collaborations with industry professionals, ensuring that our students are well-prepared for the digital age.
