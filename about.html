HISTORY OF ST. GEORGE'S

A legacy spanning over a century of Christian education, academic distinction, and service to society in the historic city of Agra.

St. George’s College, Agra Cantt. was established in the form of Cantonment College in the year 1875 named by the Most Revd. Edward Ralph Johnson, Agra. The Bishop of Calcutta being its First Chairman. The Chaplain of St. George’s Church, Agra was its First Secretary and the Principal. The College is now being run under the auspices of the Diocese of Agra, Church of North India.

The Management and the Principal, realising the pressing demand of education of the society, came up with the idea of St. George’s School, Unit-II at Bagh Muzaffar Khan, centrally located, stretching over acres of land with a grand building, well equipped classrooms, labs and library and spacious playgrounds; a Pride of Agra.

The school was founded on 22nd April 2003 with the blessings from the Chairman the Rt. Revd. S.R. Cutting, Bishop of Diocese of Agra and the Founder Principal Mr. J.S. Jeremiah. Today the school is led by the visionary Principal Mr. Ashish Paul Habil.

Aegis & CISCE Council Affiliation

St. George’s School Unit-II functions under the AEGIS of Diocese of Agra, Diocese Education Board, 4/116-B, Church Road, Civil Lines, Agra. The school is affiliated to the Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations (CISCE), New Delhi with school code UP386.

Guiding Philosophy • Institutional Creed

1. VISION

St. George’s School Unit II is dedicated to imparting holistic, high-quality education grounded in sincerity, dedication, and self-confidence. We strive to cultivate everlasting values that empower students to realize their fullest potential while inspiring a spirit of selflessness, empathy, and service toward making the world a better place.

Guided by an educational philosophy that seeks to inspire rather than dictate, and to understand rather than condemn, our institution nurtures an environment of mutual respect, harmony, and peace. Through teamwork, professional excellence, open communication, and leading by personal example, we uphold the foundational belief that vital to the quality of life is our ability to work together, learn from one another, and grow collectively toward excellence.

Core Values: Sincerity Dedication Self-Confidence Empathy & Service Mutual Respect Harmony & Peace Collective Excellence
Foundational Framework

STRATEGIC PILLARS OF THE VISION

To fully capture the depth and intent of the refined vision statement, the core aspirations of St. George’s School can be understood through four foundational pillars:

1. Holistic & Value-Based Education

Pillar 01
  • Total Education: Emphasizes balanced development across academic, emotional, social, and physical dimensions.
  • Everlasting Values: Focuses on instilling integrity, self-confidence, and ethical grounding to help students realize their highest potential.
  • Selflessness & Service: Encourages students and educators alike to cultivate an "Other-Centred" mindset dedicated to positive societal impact.

2. Inspirational & Compassionate Pedagogy

Pillar 02
  • Inspiration Over Dictation: Shift from authoritative teaching to mentorship that sparks curiosity and intrinsic motivation.
  • Understanding Over Condemnation: Foster a restorative, empathetic learning climate where mistakes are treated as vital learning opportunities.
  • Sensitivity & Inclusivity: Increase awareness of individual needs and backgrounds, creating a safe space for every learner.

3. Collaborative & Professional Culture

Pillar 03
  • Teamwork & Mutual Acceptance: Build a strong sense of community among staff and students by recognizing and supporting individual strengths and weaknesses.
  • Leading by Example: Cultivate courtesy, refined manners, and professional dedication across all levels of the institution.
  • Openness to Growth: Maintain an environment that actively invites constructive feedback and continuous self-improvement.

4. Harmonious Environment & Pursuit of Excellence

Pillar 04
  • Peace & Co-operation: Ensure the campus remains a sanctuary of safety, respect, and collaborative spirit.
  • Collective Elevation: Reinforce the conviction that personal and institutional quality of life thrives when individuals learn together and uplift one another.
