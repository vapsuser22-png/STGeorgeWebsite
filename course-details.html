Senior Secondary Science (ISC XI & XII)

Rigorous foundation in pure sciences preparing Georgians for premier engineering, medical, and technological universities:

  • PCMB: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology & English
  • PCMC: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Computer Science & English
  • Hands-on experiments in dedicated Physics, Chemistry & Biology Labs
  • Competitive exam guidance for NEET, JEE & CUET
View Lab & Library Gallery

Senior Secondary Commerce (ISC XI & XII)

Specialized commerce track fostering business acumen, financial literacy, and corporate leadership:

  • Core Subjects: Economics, Commerce, Accountancy, Business Studies & English
  • Real-world market analytics, ledger maintenance, and corporate case studies
  • Preparation for Chartered Accountancy (CA), CS, Management & Law
  • Interactive smart boards for financial data presentation
Explore Academic Streams

High School Curriculum (ICSE Grades IX & X)

Comprehensive national curriculum under CISCE New Delhi focusing on depth, inquiry, and analytical competence:

  • Group I: English Literature & Language, Hindi, History, Civics & Geography
  • Group II: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology
  • Group III: Computer Applications, Physical Education, Art
  • Internal assessment, continuous evaluation, and project exhibitions
High School Wing

Primary School Curriculum (Grades I to V)

Holistic foundational learning nurturing curiosity, bilingual expression, and joyful discovery:

  • Core English, Hindi, Mathematics & Environmental Studies (EVS)
  • Interactive smart panels for experiential visual lessons
  • Creative arts, fancy dress competitions & music synthesis
  • Structured physical training, sack races & junior calisthenics
Primary School Wing

Co-Curricular & Athletic Programs

Balanced development instilling team spirit, resilience, and camaraderie across four houses:

  • Four House System: Red, Blue, Green, Yellow competing for Champion House Trophy
  • CISCE Regional Football Tournaments, Athletics & National Sports Day
  • Annual Fete, cultural stage festivals & exhibitions
  • Music academy with guitar & electronic synthesizer training
