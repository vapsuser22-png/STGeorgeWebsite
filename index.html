Institutional Legacy & Vision

History

A Distinguished Legacy of Education in Agra

St. George’s College, Agra Cantt. was established in the form of Cantonment College in the year 1875 named by the Most Revd. Edward Ralph Johnson, Agra. The Bishop of Calcutta being its First Chairman. The Chaplain of St. George’s Church, Agra was its First Secretary and the Principal. The College is now being run under the auspices of the Diocese of Agra, Church of North India.

The Management and the Principal, realising the pressing demand education of the society, came up with the idea of St. George’s School, Unit-II at Bagh Muzaffar Khan, centrally located, stretching over acres of land with a grand building, well equipped classrooms, labs and library and spacious playgrounds; a Pride of Agra.

The school was founded on 22nd April 2003 with the blessings from the Chairman the Rt. Revd. S.R. Cutting, Bishop of Diocese of Agra and the Founder Principal Mr. J.S. Jeremiah. Today the school is led by the visionary Principal Mr. Ashish Paul Habil.

St. George’s School Unit-II functions under the AEGIS of Diocese of Agra, Diocese Education Board, 4/116-B, Church Road, Civil lines, Agra. The school is affiliated to the Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations (CISCE), New Delhi with school code UP386.

Vision

“St. George’s School Unit II is dedicated to imparting holistic, high-quality education grounded in sincerity, dedication, and self-confidence. We strive to cultivate everlasting values that empower students to realize their fullest potential while inspiring a spirit of selflessness, empathy, and service toward making the world a better place. Guided by an educational philosophy that seeks to inspire rather than dictate, and to understand rather than condemn, our institution nurtures an environment of mutual respect, harmony, and peace. Through teamwork, professional excellence, open communication, and leading by personal example, we uphold the foundational belief that vital to the quality of life is our ability to work together, learn from one another, and grow collectively toward excellence.”

Strategic Pillars of the Vision

To fully capture the depth and intent of the refined vision statement, the core aspirations of St. George’s School can be understood through four foundational pillars:

01

Holistic & Value-Based Education

Total Education: Emphasizes balanced development across academic, emotional, social, and physical dimensions.
Everlasting Values: Focuses on instilling integrity, self-confidence, and ethical grounding to help students realize their highest potential.
Selflessness & Service: Encourages students and educators alike to cultivate an “Other-Centred” mindset dedicated to positive societal impact.
02

Inspirational & Compassionate Pedagogy

Inspiration Over Dictation: Shift from authoritative teaching to mentorship that sparks curiosity and intrinsic motivation.
Understanding Over Condemnation: Foster a restorative, empathetic learning climate where mistakes are treated as vital learning opportunities.
Sensitivity & Inclusivity: Increase awareness of individual needs and backgrounds, creating a safe space for every learner.
03

Collaborative & Professional Culture

Teamwork & Mutual Acceptance: Build a strong sense of community among staff and students by recognizing and supporting individual strengths and weaknesses.
Leading by Example: Cultivate courtesy, refined manners, and professional dedication across all levels of the institution.
Openness to Growth: Maintain an environment that actively invites constructive feedback and continuous self-improvement.
04

Harmonious Environment & Pursuit of Excellence

Peace & Co-operation: Ensure the campus remains a sanctuary of safety, respect, and collaborative spirit.
Collective Elevation: Reinforce the conviction that personal and institutional quality of life thrives when individuals learn together and uplift one another.

Admission Procedure

Discipline & Norms

School Guidelines

Students Guidelines

Code of Conduct
  • 1. Carefully abide by the rules and regulations of school. Take part in all school activities.
  • 2. Students should not bring outsiders/friends/relatives in the school campus.
  • 3. Speak only English during class hours except in Hindi and Sanskrit class.
  • 4. Be polite to friends and respectful to teachers.
  • 5. Do the home work/project/Holiday home work/class work assignment on time.
  • 6. Keep school property neat and clean. Damages to school property may impose fine and suspension from school.
  • 7. Be punctual and not go out of the school without Principal/coordinator permission.
  • 8. No half day is permissible in school so parents should not come to fetch children during the school hours.
  • 9. Students come to school in neat and clean uniform, short haircut and clean and polished shoes. “Press Onwards”.
  • 10. Only Bicycles are allowed to be parked in school premises. Students are not allowed to commute to school by Bike or scooter under any circumstances.
  • 11. Students should not eat or buy food or any other items from outside the school gate.
  • 12. Students should not talk or mix with any stranger and report immediately to the class teacher if there is any issue during the school hours.

Parents Guidelines

Parent Partnership
Parent Responsibilities

Parents are requested to ensure and see that:

  • Their wards come to school regularly, punctually and smartly dressed.
  • Their diaries and notebooks are properly maintained.
  • They do their homework / assignments / projects regularly.
  • They should not wear jewellery or gold rings or ear rings, steel or any type of bangle and should not bring any expensive item including mobile phones, watches, money etc. which is not relevant for school and studies.
  • Candles, Match box, Lighter or any item which could be used to generate fire, chemicals, steel ruler, knives, medicines or any such dangerous item which may be harmful for health should not be brought to school.
  • All boys must keep their hair short (except Sikh boys) and girls must tie up their hair neatly with red ribbons. No student is allowed to wear hair colour, mehndi or nail polish while attending school.
Parent Communication & PTM

Teacher Remarks: Remarks and observations recorded by teachers in the anecdotal record and school diary must be regularly checked.
Parent Signatures: Parents are required to review and sign these records during Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTM).

Pickup, Drop-off & Transportation

Pickup Timing: Parents must fetch their children within 15 minutes after school finishes.
Transport Details: Submit details of individual, auto, or van driver responsible for pickup and drop-off.
Parent Authentication: The submitted transport details must be duly signed and authenticated by the parents.

Student Conduct & Participation

Mandatory Activities: Active participation in games and other school activities is required.

Food & Nutrition

Tiffin Box Requirements: Only healthy, nutritious food and drinks must be packed in the student's tiffin box.
